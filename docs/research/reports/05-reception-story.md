# Colony 28 - reception and story research

Date of research: 2026-10-16. Info is thin: this is a 1997 Czech DOS game re-released on Steam in Feb 2026 with almost no community footprint.

## Key facts
- Steam page: https://store.steampowered.com/app/4381340/Colony_28/ - released 2026-02-20, EUR 4.99 (search snippet said $3.99), dev "5th Dimension", publisher "Jindrich Skeldal", Czech-only interface/audio/subtitles, tags: Action, Arcade, 2D Platformer, 2D, 1990s, Retro, Single-player.
- Original: 1997 MS-DOS side-scrolling platformer by Napoleon Games, published by JRC Interactive (June 1997). Made by team 5th Dimension led by Martin Matejka, who later joined Jindrich Rohlik's Napoleon Games. Inspired by Another World and Flashback. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colony_28
- Steam blurb: "authentic 1996 Czech arcade-adventure" (note: 1996 on store vs 1997 on Wikipedia), "dark industrial visuals and haunting soundtrack", "8 sprawling levels", puzzle-solving with keycards and traps. Source: store page above.

## Story / setting
- Wikipedia: aliens invaded Earth in 2013, conquered it in weeks, humans enslaved/converted into obedient androids, Earth renamed "Colony 28". Protagonist: cyborg/combat robot who keeps his memories due to a malfunction, turns on aliens, helps the human resistance. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colony_28
- Steam page (as extracted) says set in 2028 on devastated Earth - conflicts with Wikipedia's 2013 invasion date (inferred: 2028 may be the present-day of the story or a store-page extraction error; unverified).
- Pre-rendered video cutscenes between stages; finale is a first-person 3D dungeon-crawl section and an FMV climax instead of a classic boss fight. Source: https://kimimithegameeatingshemonster.com/2026/03/06/colony-28-a-czech-wonder-finally-released-worldwide/
- Protagonist is a "yellow robot" (Steam review, see below).

## Press and blogs
- Only one found: blog review "Colony 28: a Czech wonder finally released worldwide" (2026-03-06), https://kimimithegameeatingshemonster.com/2026/03/06/colony-28-a-czech-wonder-finally-released-worldwide/ (fetched via summarizer):
  - Praise: free exploration of stages in any direction; cover mechanic gives combat rhythm; creative environmental puzzles (acid on robots, observing passcodes, redirecting grenade blasts); defeated enemies stay gone so puzzles can be solved safely afterward; cutscenes make small areas feel part of a bigger world; FMV finale.
  - Difficulty: "combat always feels nerve-racking" - limited ammo, high enemy damage; melee alternative.
  - Complaint: frequent crashes, especially when loading saves, called "an unforgivable stumble in a commercial release".
- 1997 contemporary: Petr Bulir (Level magazine) gave 60%, praised atmosphere, music, story and ending; criticized animations and graphics; noted high difficulty and Flashback similarity. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colony_28
- Wikipedia: "mostly positive" reception.
