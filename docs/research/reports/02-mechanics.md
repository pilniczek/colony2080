# Colony 28 - gameplay mechanics and controls

## Key finding
The Steam game is NOT a new title. It is a Steam re-release (2026-02-20) of the 1997 Czech MS-DOS side-scrolling action-platformer "Colony 28" (5th Dimension / Napoleon Games). It is a Another World / Flashback / Blackthorne-style cinematic platformer. There is NO colony-building or management layer (the name refers to Earth renamed "Colony 28" by aliens). Inferred: Steam build likely wraps the DOS game (Pentium II / Win10 requirements, 500MB, Windows/Mac/Linux, Czech only).

## Facts (with sources)
- Steam: release 2026-02-20, dev 5th Dimension, publisher Jindrich Skeldal, Czech-only text and audio, tags Action/Arcade/2D Platformer/1990's/Retro/Singleplayer, 8 levels (colony sectors), keycards + hazards. https://store.steampowered.com/app/4381340/Colony_28/ ; https://steambase.io/games/colony-28/info (price $3.99, Windows/Mac/Linux, Family Sharing)
- Premise: Earth conquered by aliens in 2013, renamed Colony 28; humans turned into obedient androids; protagonist is a cyborg whose control chip short-circuits when transport is attacked, regains free will, joins resistance. Goal (per LaunchBox): destroy the alien mothership. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colony_28 ; https://gamesdb.launchbox-app.com/games/details/85424-colony-28
- Inspirations: Another World, Flashback; cover mechanic added in full version like Blackthorne. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colony_28
- Structure: task-based objectives per level; completing them advances to next level. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colony_28. Multiple interconnected areas explorable non-linearly, in-game map, pre-rendered video transitions between areas, ends with a first-person 3D dungeon segment. https://kimimithegameeatingshemonster.com/2026/03/06/colony-28-a-czech-wonder-finally-released-worldwide/
- Combat: robotic arm with machine gun using three ammo types; limited ammo; one ammo crate per stage; secondary special powerful limited weapons found in stages; infinite punch/kick melee (weaker). https://gamesdb.launchbox-app.com/games/details/85424-colony-28 ; kimimi blog above
- "Hide and Shoot": press a key to duck into the background, fully invulnerable, may stay indefinitely. Kimimi blog above; https://www.myabandonware.com/game/colony-28-dl9 (description mentions ducking into background)
- Enemies: placed in fixed locations, stay dead once killed (no respawn), high damage per hit, react to player position/movement. Kimimi blog
- Items: inventory; limited health pickups; keycards; puzzle items. Puzzles: acid on robots, watching enemy actions through crawlspaces, grenades to open paths, contextual item use. Kimimi blog; Steam page
- Save: manual save at any time. Crashes reported, especially on loading saves (original/DOS-era version). Kimimi blog
- Difficulty: high. Review criticism of difficulty incl. a 12-second timed segment (set charges, run to elevator). Wikipedia reviewer (Petr Bulir) 60%, high difficulty, praised atmosphere/music/story, criticised animations/graphics. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colony_28 ; Steam review: "absolutely kicked my butt at times", yellow robot protagonist (Steam reviews API, 1 review only)
- Steam community: threads "Gamepad" (6 replies, content not retrievable), "GFX filter", "Nostalgie". https://steamcommunity.com/app/4381340/discussions/ . Inference: gamepad support/graphics filter exist or are requested in Steam build.
