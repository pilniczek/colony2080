About This Game

Colony 28 is an original Czech arcade-adventure from 1996. It is the very first game developed by Karel Matějka (Ko.dll), the author of the phenomenal BZZZT. Please keep in mind that this is not "retro"—it is a truly old game. Not to mention, it is only available in the Czech language.

Relive a cult classic of Czech sci-fi gaming history

The year is 2028. Earth is in ruins, and the remnants of humanity struggle to survive in the harsh environments of space colonies. After an alien invasion, humankind was transformed into a working android army dedicated to draining the planet's resources. You wake up inside the chassis of a combat droid on Colony 28 (Earth), but your mind remains human. You are the final hope for the resistance against a tyrannical reign of machines and corporations.

Gameplay

Colony 28 is a classic 2D action platformer known for its gripping atmosphere and challenging gameplay. Experience the journey of a soul trapped in steel, fighting to reclaim a future for mankind.

Key Features:

  • Authentic Retro Sci-Fi: Immerse yourself in dark industrial visuals and a haunting soundtrack that defines the golden era of 90s Eastern European game dev.

  • Tactical Combat: Blast your way through mechanical sentries, but stay sharp—finding keycards and navigating hazardous traps is just as vital as your trigger finger.

  • Intricate Level Design: Explore the sprawling, labyrinthine sectors of a desolate mining colony.

  • A Piece of Gaming History: Originally released in the Czech Republic, this classic returns, optimized for modern PC players.
